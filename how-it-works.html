The process

Your account. Our algorithm. Full visibility.

The setup is deliberately simple, and deliberately built so that you never hand your money to us. Here is exactly what happens, step by step.

Free consultation

A 20–30 minute call (or a visit to our Dubai office). We discuss your capital, goals and risk tolerance, show you live accounts and recommend a strategy or a split.

Open your broker account

You open a trading account in your own name with a regulated MT5 broker and complete their KYC. We guide you through it, but the account belongs to you. Open your account →

Deposit with the broker

You fund the account directly with the broker — never with us. Minimum to start: $1,000 ($3,000 for the gold arbitrage strategy).

We connect the algorithm

You share trading-only credentials. Our team links the strategy to your account and it begins executing automatically. Nothing to install on your side.

Track live in MT5

Install the free MetaTrader 5 app on your phone or desktop, log in with your account and watch every trade, balance and equity change in real time.

Monthly reporting

Each month you receive a broker-generated statement plus a plain-English summary from us of how the strategy performed and why.

Withdraw anytime

Profits or capital — request a withdrawal from the broker whenever you want. No lock-in, no notice period, no exit penalty.

Why this structure

Built so you never have to trust us with your money.

Most "managed trading" offers ask you to send funds to the company. We think that's backwards. In our model the money never leaves your own regulated broker account, and the only permission we hold is the ability to place trades.

  • We cannot withdraw. Trading-only access means we can open and close positions — nothing else.
  • You can revoke access instantly. Change your trading password and the connection stops. You're always in control.
  • Independent records. Your statements come from the broker, so performance can't be edited by anyone — including us.
  • Regulated counterparty. Your funds sit with a regulated broker, subject to its client-money rules.

New to all this?

The whole thing, explained simply.

No trading experience needed. If you can use a banking app, you can do this. Here are the six things people ask us first, in plain language.

What is a trading algorithm?

It's a computer program that buys and sells in the financial markets automatically, following a fixed set of rules. Think of it like autopilot on a plane: it watches the markets 24 hours a day, makes decisions in milliseconds, and never gets tired, greedy or scared. You don't need to understand the rules — the same way you don't need to understand a car engine to drive.

What is "Forex"?

Forex simply means exchanging one currency for another — dollars for euros, pounds for yen. It's the biggest market in the world, open 24 hours a day, five days a week. Currency prices move up and down constantly. Our forex algorithm buys a currency when it expects the price to rise and sells when it expects it to fall — thousands of small, calculated trades that add up over the month.

What is "arbitrage"?

Arbitrage means buying something in one place and selling it in another place where it costs more, at the same moment — and keeping the difference. Like buying gold at one shop for $100 and selling it next door for $103. Our arbitrage algorithm does this with gold: it buys gold at today's price and sells a gold contract for future delivery at a slightly higher price, at the same moment — and keeps the gap. Over and over, automatically. Because it buys and sells at the same time, it doesn't matter whether the market goes up or down.

How do I make money?

You put money into a trading account that is in your own name. We connect the algorithm to it. The algorithm trades, and the profit appears in your account balance. Example: with $10,000 in the system, a 5–8% month means roughly $500 – $800 of profit added to your account that month. Below $10,000 the range is 3–5%. You can withdraw the profit, or leave it in so next month's percentage works on a bigger amount — and as your balance grows past the threshold, your rate moves up too.

Where is my money? Is it safe with you?

Your money is never with us. It stays in your own account with a regulated broker (a broker is a licensed company that gives you access to the markets — like a bank for trading). We only get permission to place trades — we cannot withdraw a single dollar. You can see your balance any time on your phone, and only you can take money out.

What do I actually have to do?

Three things, once: (1) have a short call with us, (2) open your trading account and deposit your money — we guide you through every screen, (3) install a free app called MT5 on your phone to watch it work. After that, nothing. The algorithm runs by itself. You check your app when you like, and message us on WhatsApp whenever you have a question.

The honest part. The percentages above are what the strategies have done historically — they are not a promise. Some months will be better, some worse, and a losing month is possible. Never invest money you may need soon or can't afford to lose. We'll say this on the call too.
