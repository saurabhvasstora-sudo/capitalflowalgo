Gold Spot–Futures Arbitrage

5 – 8% / month · historical

Buys gold in the spot market and simultaneously sells the gold futures contract, locking in the price difference ("basis"). Because the two legs hedge each other, the strategy does not depend on the market going up or down.

  • Hedged — no directional exposure
  • Consistent, lower-volatility monthly accrual
  • Ideal for capital preservation with yield
  • Minimum capital: $1,000

Forex Multi-Pair Algo

15 – 20% / month · historical

A systematic model trading a diversified basket of major and minor currency pairs around the clock, five days a week. Entries, exits, position sizing and risk limits are all rule-based — no human emotion.

  • Diversified across multiple currency pairs
  • Hard per-trade and daily drawdown limits
  • Higher return target with active risk control
