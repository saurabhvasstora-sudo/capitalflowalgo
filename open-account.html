What happens next

Four short steps, and we're with you on WhatsApp for every one of them.

Sign up on the platform using the link above. It takes a few minutes and is fully online — no paperwork.

Upload your passport or Emirates ID to the platform. This is the platform's own check, not ours. Usually approved within a day.
