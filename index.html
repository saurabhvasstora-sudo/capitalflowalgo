Here's the whole idea in four steps.

A trading algorithm is a computer program that buys and sells in the markets automatically, following proven rules — like autopilot. Here is how you use one.

You deposit into your account

You open a trading account in your own name with a regulated broker and put your money there. Not with us — with the broker. Only you can take it out. Start here →

We switch on the algorithm

We connect our trading program to your account. It has permission to trade, and nothing else. Takes us a few minutes, takes you zero effort.

It trades for you, 24/5

The program watches the markets every second and makes thousands of small, calculated trades. No emotions, no sleep, no guessing.

You watch the profit grow

Profit lands in your account balance. Check it any time on the free MT5 phone app. Withdraw whenever you want, or leave it in to grow faster.
