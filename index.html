Questions

The questions everyone asks first

Where is my money held? Can Capital Flow Algo withdraw it?

Your money is held in your own trading account, in your name, with a regulated MT5 broker. We connect the algorithm with trading-only permissions. We cannot withdraw, transfer or move your funds — only you can.

Are the returns guaranteed?

No. The 5–8% and 15–20% figures are historical monthly ranges from live trading, not guarantees. Markets change, and losing months are possible. Please read our Risk Disclosure before investing.

Can I stop or withdraw at any time?

Yes. There is no lock-in period. You can pause the strategy or withdraw funds from your broker account at any time, subject to open trades being closed.

Did you build these algorithms yourselves?

No — and we're upfront about that. We partner with specialist quantitative developers and license their strategies. Our role is to vet them on our own capital, monitor them daily, and give you honest, transparent access.
