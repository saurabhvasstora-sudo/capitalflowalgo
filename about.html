About Capital Flow Algo

Access to serious algorithms, without the black box.

We started Capital Flow Algo because high-quality systematic strategies were reserved for funds and family offices, while private investors were left with signal groups and promises. We wanted a third option: vetted algorithms, your own account, and complete visibility.

Our story

Built in Dubai, built on transparency.

Capital Flow Algo operates from Dubai Silicon Oasis, one of the UAE's technology free zones. We are a small, focused team of traders and technologists whose job is to find automated strategies that actually hold up in live markets — and then to make them accessible on fair terms.

We don't claim to have written every line of code ourselves. Our edge is in selection and oversight: partnering with specialist quantitative developers, testing each strategy with our own money before any client's, monitoring execution daily, and reporting honestly — including the months that don't go to plan.

Today we run an integrated system of automated strategies for clients, and we are continually evaluating more to add to it. Every one of them trades inside the client's own account with a regulated broker. That structure isn't a feature; it's the whole point.

Principles

What we hold ourselves to

Never touch client money

Funds stay in your own broker account. We hold trading permissions only. If we disappeared tomorrow, your money wouldn't.

Our capital goes first

No strategy reaches a client until it has traded live on our own money for months. We eat our own cooking, every time.

Ranges, not guarantees

We publish historical ranges and the months behind them. We will never tell you a return is guaranteed, because it isn't.

Radical visibility

Live MT5 access from day one. Broker-issued statements. Investor logins for anyone who wants to verify before investing.

Honest about origin

We license strategies from specialist developers and say so. Pretending otherwise would be the first lie — and there wouldn't be a last.

A real office, real people

We're in Dubai Silicon Oasis and we'd rather meet you than message you. Come and see how it works before you decide.

Broker partnerships

Who your account is actually opened with.

We are an introducing partner to the trading platforms below. That is an important distinction: we introduce you, and the account is opened in your own name, directly with the platform. They hold your money and process your deposits and withdrawals — we only place trades. You can verify our partner code with either platform directly.

We publish the partner codes so you can check them, and we have requested signed confirmation letters from both platforms — when they arrive they will be published here in full. We would rather show you a pending note than a document you cannot verify.

How we compare

The questions worth asking anyone who offers you an algorithm.

We are not the only people selling automated trading. We are one of very few who will answer all eight of these the same way in public. Ask whoever else you are speaking to — the answers tell you more than any performance figure.

Where does my money sit? In a trading account in your own name. We never touch it. "Send it to our account and we'll trade it for you."
Can you withdraw my funds? No. We hold trading permissions only — withdrawals are yours alone. Full access, often including withdrawal rights.

Can I leave whenever I want? Any time. No lock-in, no notice period, no exit fee. Change your password and we're disconnected. Lock-in periods, notice requirements, exit penalties.
Where's the risk disclosure? Published in full, linked from every page, written in plain English. Buried, generic, or missing entirely.
Who do I actually speak to? A person on WhatsApp, same day, who knows your account. A chatbot, a reseller, or nobody after you've paid.
