Safety of funds

Where is my money held?

In your own trading account, opened in your name with a regulated MT5 broker. Capital Flow Algo never receives, holds or transfers client money.

Can Capital Flow Algo withdraw my funds?

No. We operate with trading-only permissions. Deposits and withdrawals can only be made by you, the account holder, through the broker's own verified channels.

What if I want to stop?

Change your account's trading password (or ask the broker to reset it) and our connection ends immediately. You can then withdraw your full balance from the broker at any time. There is no lock-in and no exit fee.

What happens if Capital Flow Algo ceased operating?

Nothing happens to your money. It remains in your own broker account, untouched, and you keep full control. This is the main reason we chose the no-custody model.

Returns & risk

How is the 3–8% monthly figure calculated?

It is the net monthly return actually recorded on live accounts — after spreads, commissions and swap costs — over the period shown on our Performance page. Nothing is modelled or back-tested. The range reflects both the natural month-to-month variation of the markets and your capital tier, since larger accounts run a fuller allocation. As with any trading, individual months land inside, at the edges of, or occasionally outside that band.

Have you had losing months?

Drawdowns are a normal part of any trading strategy, and the forex algorithm in particular is designed to accept short-term losses in pursuit of higher returns. Our full month-by-month record, including weaker months, is on the Performance page, and we will show you live accounts on request.

Why does my monthly percentage depend on how much I invest?

A larger balance lets the system allocate across more of its strategies at a fuller position size, so the blended monthly range improves. Below $10,000 the historical range is 3–5% per month; from that point upward it is 5–8%. Your tier is reviewed as the balance grows, including growth from profits you leave in.

Which strategy is lower risk?

The gold spot–futures arbitrage strategy. It is fully hedged and does not depend on market direction, which is why its returns are lower and steadier. The forex algorithm targets higher returns and carries higher variance. Many clients combine the two.

What does "market neutral" actually mean?

It means the strategy does not need the market to go up or down to make money. In our gold arbitrage engine, every purchase of gold is matched by an equal sale of a gold futures contract at the same moment. If gold crashes, one side loses and the other gains almost exactly the same amount — what we keep is the price gap between them, which was locked in the second both trades were placed. Direction becomes irrelevant; the spread is the product.

What is leverage, and do you use it?

Leverage lets a trading account control a position larger than its cash balance — useful, but it magnifies losses as well as gains. Our arbitrage engine uses it only to balance the two sides of a hedged trade, which is a very different thing from taking a leveraged directional bet. The forex engine scales position size to account equity and current volatility, with a stop-loss on every trade and a daily loss limit that halts trading automatically.

What happens to my account during a market crash?

The arbitrage engine is largely unaffected, because its two sides offset each other — crashes tend to widen spreads, which can help it. The forex engine reduces exposure automatically when volatility spikes and around scheduled high-impact news. Neither is immune: gap moves over a weekend can jump past a stop-loss. This is precisely why we publish live account logins rather than asking you to imagine how it behaves.

How often does the algorithm trade?

It varies with market conditions rather than following a fixed schedule. The forex engine typically places multiple trades a day across its basket of pairs; the arbitrage engine holds positions until the spread converges, which can be days or weeks. You will see every one of them appear in your MT5 app in real time.

Can I lose more than I invest?

Our partner brokers offer negative-balance protection on retail accounts, meaning your account cannot go below zero. You could, however, lose some or all of the capital you deposit. Only invest money you can afford to put at risk.

Understanding the basics

I have never traded before. Is this suitable for me?

Yes — no trading knowledge is required, because you never place a trade. Your only tasks are opening the account, funding it, and installing the free MT5 app if you want to watch. That said, "no experience needed" is not the same as "no risk": you should understand that the value of your account will move up and down, and that losing periods happen. Our plain-English guide explains everything in ordinary language.

What is MetaTrader 5, and why do you use it?

MT5 is the industry-standard trading platform, used by brokers and funds worldwide. We use it for one reason above all: it produces an independent, unalterable record of every trade. You see exactly what we see, at the same moment, on your own phone — we cannot edit, delay or dress up a single entry.

What is the difference between an investor password and a master password?

An MT5 account has two passwords. The master password allows trading and account changes. The investor password is read-only: it shows every trade, balance and equity change but cannot place, close or alter anything. The logins we publish on this site are investor passwords, which is why we can share them publicly without risk. When you become a client, you give us trading-only access and keep your own withdrawal credentials private.

What is the "spread" between spot and futures?

The spot price is what gold costs to buy today. A futures contract is an agreement to sell it at a set price on a future date, and that price is usually slightly higher, because it accounts for storage and financing costs. That difference is the "basis". As the contract approaches its date, the two prices converge — and the gap that was locked in at the start becomes the profit. It is one of the oldest strategies in commodity markets.

Why can't I just run an algorithm myself?

You can buy plenty of bots online, and most of them lose money. The difficulty is not obtaining code — it is knowing which models survive live conditions, monitoring them daily, having the capital to test them properly before risking client money, and shutting them down when market conditions change. That selection and oversight is what you are actually paying us for.

Does the algorithm run on my computer?

No. It runs on our dedicated servers and connects to your account. Your computer or phone can be switched off entirely; trading continues uninterrupted.

How do I read the MT5 screen?

Balance is your closed, settled money. Equity is balance plus or minus the profit on trades still open, so it moves constantly during the day — a lower equity than balance simply means there are open positions currently down, which is normal and not a loss until closed. History lists every completed trade. We walk you through all of it on your onboarding call.
