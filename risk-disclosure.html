Risk Disclosure Statement

Please read this statement carefully before using any service provided by Capital Flow Algo ("Capital Flow Algo", "we", "us"). By engaging our services you confirm that you have read, understood and accepted the risks described here.

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Trading leveraged financial products carries a high level of risk and may not be suitable for all investors. You could lose some or all of your invested capital. Do not invest money you cannot afford to lose.

1. No guarantee of returns

Any performance figures shown on this website — including the historical monthly ranges of approximately 3–8%, which vary according to account size tier — are historical results from live trading over the periods stated. They are provided for information only. They are not a forecast, projection, promise or guarantee of future performance. Future returns may be lower, may be zero, or may be negative. Individual results will vary depending on the timing of your entry, the size of your capital, broker conditions and market events.

2. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results

Financial markets change. A strategy that has performed well historically may perform poorly, or lose money, in the future. Market regimes, volatility, liquidity, interest rates, exchange policies and regulation can all change in ways that affect a strategy's profitability. Where figures on this website are marked as "illustrative", they are representative examples and not a record of any specific account.

3. Nature of the products traded

Our strategies trade foreign exchange (forex), derivatives (including futures and perpetual contracts) and/or digital assets. These products are typically traded with leverage. Leverage magnifies both gains and losses: a relatively small market movement can have a proportionately larger impact on your account, and losses can accumulate rapidly. Digital-asset markets in particular are highly volatile and may be subject to sudden regulatory changes, exchange outages or counterparty failure.

4. Strategy-specific risks

Gold Spot–Futures Arbitrage

  • Basis risk: the spread between spot and futures prices may narrow, disappear or invert, reducing or eliminating expected returns.
  • Execution risk: the two legs of a trade may not fill simultaneously or at expected prices, resulting in unintended exposure.
  • Counterparty risk: the failure, insolvency or suspension of a broker may result in loss.
  • Funding and financing risk: funding rates on perpetual contracts and borrowing costs can change and become unfavourable.

Forex Multi-Pair Algorithm

  • Market risk: the strategy takes directional positions and will experience losing trades, losing days and periods of drawdown.
  • Model risk: a rule-based system may underperform when market behaviour differs from the conditions under which it was developed and tested.
  • Gap and news risk: price gaps over weekends or around economic announcements may cause stop-loss orders to execute at worse prices than intended.
  • Liquidity risk: in stressed markets, spreads widen and execution quality deteriorates.

5. Automated trading and technology risk

Our strategies are executed by automated software connected to your trading account. Automated systems are subject to risks including software errors, connectivity failures, server outages, data-feed errors, broker platform malfunctions and delays. Any of these may result in trades not being placed, being placed incorrectly, or not being closed as intended, and may cause losses. While we monitor systems and maintain safeguards, no technology is infallible.
