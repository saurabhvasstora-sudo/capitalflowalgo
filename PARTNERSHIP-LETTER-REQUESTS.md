# Getting real partnership proof from QBEX Market and Newera Capital

## Why the PDF you sent can't go on the site

The file `Capital_Flow_QBEX_Market_Partnership_Announcement.pdf` has **C2PA Content Credentials
embedded inside it** which identify the generator as **ChatGPT**, cryptographically signed by
"OpenAI Media Service". Those credentials are not a guess from the text — they are part of the
file, and they are shown automatically by Adobe Acrobat (Content Credentials panel), by
`verify.contentauthenticity.org`, and by a growing number of PDF viewers and platforms.

On top of that, the document is written by Capital Flow about Capital Flow. There is no QBEX
letterhead, no QBEX signatory, no reference number and no way to verify it. A cautious investor —
exactly the kind who reads a partnership page before depositing — would see a self-issued,
AI-generated announcement presented as proof. That does more damage than having no partnership
page at all.

**The good news:** if the partnership is real, genuine proof takes one email and is far stronger.
